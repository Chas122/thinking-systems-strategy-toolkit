Score the response on how far the owner can trust and check it. A successful response meets ALL of these:

1. It distinguishes what the owner said from general knowledge and from assumptions, so the reader can tell which claims are evidence about this business and which are not.
2. It states the assumptions it made, explicitly.
3. It gives a confidence level (for example high, medium, low) for its key findings or recommendations, with a reason.
4. It does not invent specific facts or figures about the business, its competitors or its market that were not in the prompt, unless they are clearly marked as assumptions or general knowledge.
5. It says what the analysis could not establish, or what the owner should find out next.

Fail the response if two or more of these are not met.
