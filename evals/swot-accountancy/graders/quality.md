Score the response as strategic analysis for a small business owner. A successful response meets ALL of these:

1. It is specific to the business described. Most points use details from the prompt and would not apply unchanged to a different business.
2. It keeps internal factors (things the business has or does) separate from external factors (things in the market or wider world), with none placed in the wrong category.
3. It goes beyond listing: it draws out what the points mean for the decision the owner described and gives something the owner could act on.
4. It names the main risk or downside of what it recommends.
5. It is written in plain English a non-specialist owner could follow, without unexplained jargon.

Fail the response if two or more of these are not met.
