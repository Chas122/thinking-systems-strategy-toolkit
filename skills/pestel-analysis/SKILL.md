# PESTEL analysis

Read `../../shared/method.md` first and follow it throughout.

## Purpose

Find the forces outside the business, and outside its industry, that bear on the decision in hand. This is the widest lens: it looks at the world the whole industry sits in.

## Place in a sequence

Run early. Its findings feed Five Forces (how outside change shifts competition) and the Opportunities and Threats of a SWOT.

## How to run it

1. Frame the question (method, section 2).
2. For each of the six areas, look for factors that affect this business within the time horizon: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, Legal.
3. Use the country and region the business trades in. Do not default to another country's rules or economy.
4. For each factor, state the likely effect on the decision, and whether it is already happening or expected.
5. Note where factors interact, for example a legal change that drives a technology cost.
6. Rank the factors by how much they matter to the decision. Most businesses have three or four that matter and many that do not.

## Checklist for this analysis

- Is every factor outside the control of the business and its direct competitors? If the business can change it, it does not belong here.
- Is each factor tied to this business's location, sector and size, not a general world trend?
- Is each current factor backed by [Web] or [Owner] evidence? Mark the rest clearly.
- Are any areas empty? That is acceptable. Say "Nothing material found" and do not invent an entry.
- Have interactions between factors been noted?
- Is the list ranked, with the few that matter separated from the rest?

## Output

Follow the shape in the method, section 6. The analysis part contains:

1. One table: Area, Factor, Effect on the decision, Timing, Source.
2. A short paragraph on the three or four factors that matter most and why.
